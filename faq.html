Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick solutions and answers to common queries about accounts, academics, campus events, and facilities.

To reset your password:

  1. Navigate to the Login Page and click on Forgot Password?.
  2. Enter your registered university email ID (e.g., student@charusat.edu.in).
  3. Check your inbox for a secure password-reset link valid for 15 minutes.
  4. Follow the prompts to configure a new strong password complying with security policies.

All weekly lab assignments and digital code repositories should be uploaded via the Course Work tab in your Student Dashboard. Make sure your submissions are compressed in ZIP format or linked via an authorized GitHub repository before the midnight deadline.

Visit the Campus Events directory. Filter by Technical, Cultural, or Sports. Click Register on any card to confirm your participation. You can manage and track all your active registrations directly inside your Dashboard.

Go to your Student Profile and click Edit Profile. For residential address modifications or state proof, please submit supporting documentation to the Registrar Desk or email registrar@charusat.ac.in.

University regulations mandate a minimum of 80% aggregate attendance across all theory lectures and laboratory sessions to be eligible to sit for university end-semester examinations. You can monitor your live attendance percentage on your dashboard.

Connect to the SSID CHARUSAT_WIFI. When prompted in the browser captive portal, authenticate using your Student ID and University password. MAC address registration is supported at the IT Support Center, CSPIT Building Ground Floor.
